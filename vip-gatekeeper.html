A Telegram bot that checks payment receipts before you do

I built a bot that sells access to a paid Telegram channel by bank transfer. It reads every receipt with AI, turns away fake and reused ones, and only asks the owner to approve real payments. Approving takes one tap.

The problem

Many Nigerian creators sell paid Telegram groups for signals, tips, coaching or courses, and most customers pay by bank transfer. That avoids card fees, but every sale turns into manual work. The customer sends a screenshot in DMs. The owner opens it, checks the amount against the plan, confirms the money arrived, finds the person, adds them to the channel and notes down when their access expires.

That doesn't scale, and it's easy to cheat. An edited screenshot or a receipt someone already used can get through when the owner is busy.

What I built

Telegram→ Is it a receipt?→ Gemini reads it→ Valid payment?→ Used before?→ Save as pending→ Ask owner
  1. The customer picks a plan. The bot shows the plans and the bank details. There is no website and no checkout page, so everything happens inside Telegram.
  2. Only receipts go forward. If the customer sends anything other than a photo, the bot replies with the payment instructions again.
  3. AI reads the receipt. Google Gemini pulls out the amount, the sender's name, the date and the reference. If the image isn't a real transfer receipt, the customer is told straight away.
  4. Reused receipts are blocked. Each receipt is checked against every receipt already in the members sheet, so a screenshot that has been used before can't buy access twice.
  5. The owner approves with one tap. Only receipts that pass every check reach the owner. They arrive as a photo with the details already read out, plus Approve and Reject buttons.
  6. Access is granted automatically. Approval creates a single-use invite link, sends it to the customer and marks them active with a start date and an expiry date. If the owner rejects, the customer gets a polite message and the record is updated.

Tested end to end

On 16 November 2026 I ran the whole flow with a transfer receipt. A monthly payment went from photo to an active member with a 30-day expiry and an invite link. A second test took the reject path, and the customer got the rejection message. The bot is live, running on n8n.

This is a new build, so I'll add real numbers here (receipts checked, fakes caught, time saved) once paying members are going through it.

Who this is for

The same setup fits any business that sells access and gets paid by transfer, such as paid communities, signal and tips channels, coaching groups, course cohorts or member-only WhatsApp groups. Plans and prices live in a Google Sheet, so the owner can change them without touching the workflow.
